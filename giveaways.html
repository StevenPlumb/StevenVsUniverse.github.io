How Milestones Work

Subscriber milestones are community goals, not giveaway entry requirements. Reaching a milestone does not automatically open entries. Giveaway dates and official rules will be announced separately.

Subscriber counts are based on YouTube's publicly reported data and may be rounded or delayed.

Giveaway Information & Rules

Each giveaway will have its own official rules published before entries open.

  • Entry periods, eligibility, geographic restrictions, and age requirements will be stated in the official rules.
  • The entry method, winner selection process, and prize delivery details will be announced before the giveaway begins.
  • No purchase will be necessary to enter or win.
  • YouTube subscriptions, likes, and other platform engagement will not be required as giveaway entry conditions.
  • Winners will be contacted using the method specified in the official rules.
  • Giveaways are not currently open for entry.
Stay Safe

Never send money or payment information to someone claiming you won a StevenVsUniverse giveaway. Verify announcements through our official channels.
